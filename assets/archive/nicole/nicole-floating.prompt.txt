Export:
Extract 418x418 cells; zero only alpha below 0.008, retain all other alpha.
Register the upper-body alpha-cubed centroid (top 230px of the cell) at (256,175) on a 512x512 transparent working canvas.
A0..A8 are the right-to-left turn. Return uses B0,B1,B2,B3,B4,A3,B6,B7,B8: B5 was rejected because its light core moved against the turn direction.
Pose landmarks include four fixed canvas corners, head top and side bounds, golden core center, lower body and tail tip. ImageMagick Shepards distortion maps both neighboring poses to intermediate landmark positions before alpha-aware Blend composition. This reduces separate double cores/tails compared with a plain crossfade.
The tail tip has an additional 8px horizontal and 2px vertical sine wave with a 2s period; the lower body has a smaller delayed sway.
Timeline in seconds: B8 at 0; A0 at 0.4 and 3; A1..A8 from 3.125 to 4 at 0.125s increments; B0 at 4.4 and 7; return poses B1..B8 (with the A3 substitution) from 7.125 to 8 at 0.125s increments. Smoothstep blends neighboring landmark positions.
768x480 delivery canvas, working sprite scaled to 110%. Upper-body anchor (384+dx,198+dy).
For time t, dx is -132+264*smootherstep(t/3) for 0<=t<3; 132+8*sin(pi*(t-3))^2 for 3<=t<4; 132-264*smootherstep((t-4)/3) for 4<=t<7; otherwise -132-8*sin(pi*(t-7))^2. dy=10*sin(pi*t/2)+3*sin(pi*t+0.5). smootherstep(v)=v^3*(v*(v*6-15)+10).
160 RGBA frames at 50ms (20fps), 8s infinite loop. Encode with img2webp -loop 0 -lossy -q 94 -m 4 -d 50, retaining the original soft alpha. The static fallback uses A0 centered on the same 768x480 canvas.
