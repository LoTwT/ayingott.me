浅色字迹编辑：输入 artwork-dark-transparent.png，transparent_background: true。
Use case: precise-object-edit.
Asset type: transparent Nicole card artwork, LIGHT THEME edition derived from the supplied approved gold-lettered transparent artwork.
Primary request: darken ONLY the handwritten word "Nicole" from pale champagne gold to a slightly deeper, muted honey/antique gold, approximate #bd904f. It should sit comfortably on a pure white card: noticeably more readable and less glaring than the current pale cream, but still luminous warm gold rather than dark brown ink. Keep the word fairly uniform in gold hue, with restrained existing tonal variation. Smoothly blend the final e stroke back into the existing amber-gold tail connection; do not make a hard color seam.
Preserve everything else: EXACT typography, clear capital N with two tall slanted stems and one descending diagonal, cursive lowercase icole, i dot, all stroke shapes and widths, original lower-left lettering and upper-right spirit composition, positions, scale, full original 8:5 canvas and margins. The orange-gold spirit, its exact silhouette and anatomy, highlights, gradients, tail, and placement must remain unchanged. Do NOT darken or recolor the spirit. No new font, no redraw, no re-layout, no crop to foreground.
Transparency: preserve a real RGBA transparent background, alpha=0 outside the letters/stroke/spirit and in all open spaces. No paper, white fill, texture, opaque rectangle, halo, shadow, fringe, added border, tape, particles, extra text or objects. Output only the transparent full-canvas edited artwork. This is a small lettering color adjustment, not a redesign.

深色透明画稿提取：输入为历史深色纸底试稿 artwork-dark.png（已清理），transparent_background: true。
Use case: background-extraction.
Asset type: transparent full-canvas Nicole cover artwork for an existing website card.
Input image 1: exact edit target and visual truth. Preserve the entire existing wordmark and spirit pixel-faithfully wherever possible.
Primary request: remove ALL background paper, paper color, paper texture and any background illumination, leaving ONLY the handwritten word Nicole, the thin connected rising amber stroke, and the golden-orange spirit on a genuinely transparent RGBA background. The real website provides its own paper background.
Composition: preserve the original full landscape 8:5 canvas, original object positions, sizes, margins and lower-left word / upper-right spirit diagonal. Do NOT trim to the foreground bounds, zoom, center the foreground, add margins, move any letters or redraw the spirit.
Invariants: preserve the exact clear uppercase N (two tall slanted stems and descending diagonal), the lowercase cursive icole, the i dot, the exact stroke from final e to the spirit's pointed tail, spirit anatomy, silhouette, internal light and shadow gradients and exact text color from the supplied image. The whole word must read Nicole. This is background removal, not new logo design.
Transparency: actual alpha=0 everywhere outside the foreground, including ALL letter counters and open spaces between curved spirit ribbons. Smooth anti-aliased edges without beige, white, gray, brown or black paper fringe. Keep intended gold/amber highlights belonging to the spirit; remove the paper underneath them. No checkerboard baked into pixels, no opaque rectangle, no paper remnants, no new drop shadow, halo, glow, border, tape or texture.
Output: high-resolution transparent PNG, full original composition.

导出：
ImageMagick 等比缩小源画稿，再居中裁齐至 1280×800；不将缩放后的素材称为原生分辨率。
magick artwork-light-gold-transparent.png -resize '1280x800^' -background none -gravity center -extent 1280x800 -define webp:method=6 -define webp:alpha-quality=100 -quality 92 nicole-cover-light.webp
magick artwork-dark-transparent.png -resize '1280x800^' -background none -gravity center -extent 1280x800 -define webp:method=6 -define webp:alpha-quality=100 -quality 92 nicole-cover-dark.webp
注意 -extent 前显式设置 -background none，避免将透明像素合成到默认白色画布；导出后检查四角透明。正式页面只引用 WebP，源 PNG、参考图与本记录位于 public 外。
